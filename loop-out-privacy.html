LOOP OUT

Privacy policy  ·  last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

Loop Out: Wheelie Rider has no accounts, and the developer does not collect or store any personal information himself. The Android app shows advertisements between runs, provided by Google AdMob, and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count players and runs; Google collects some data to do both. The web version of the game has no advertising and no analytics.

Advertising (Android app)

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the app may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices this may be used to show personalised ads. Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before any ads are loaded, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings → Ads → Privacy choices in the game. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your device's Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Analytics (Android app)

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase so the developer can see how many people are playing, how many runs they make and how far they get. For that Google's analytics software in the app collects an app instance identifier (a random number generated when the app is installed, which you can reset by reinstalling the app), your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and events such as the app being opened and a run starting or ending with its difficulty and distance. The developer only sees aggregated reports. Google handles this data under its own policy (How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics stays off when you decline the consent form shown at the first start.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your best distances, the difficulty you last chose and your sound and vibration settings in the app's local storage on your own device. This game data never leaves your device (only the distance of each run is reported to analytics, as described above). You can erase the records from Settings in the game, and everything is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

The Android app uses vibration (for crash feedback) and keeps the screen awake while you play, and uses the internet connection and the advertising ID to load ads and send analytics. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The Android app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the game's Google Play store page.
