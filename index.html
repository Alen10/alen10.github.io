Wheelie Bike Rider · a dirt bike wheelie game for Android

Keep the front wheel up and ride as far as you can. Gas and brake, nothing else. Lean too far back and you loop out.

Get it on Google Play Free. Plays offline. No account needed.

How to play a wheelie

  • Hold gas on the right side of the screen to lift the front wheel of the motorbike.
  • Tap brake on the left side to bring it back down before you tip over.
  • Distance only counts while the front wheel is in the air. Put it down and that wheelie is over.
  • Go too far back and you loop out. Tap gas to ride again.

Why you will keep playing

  • Pure skill. No upgrades, no coins, no energy timers. Only your balance.
  • Real bike physics with working suspension, so every wheelie feels different.
  • One record to beat. The main menu shows your best run, and a track on screen shows how close you are.
  • Instant retry: one tap and you are riding again.
  • Speed and distance in km/h or mph.
  • Optional synth soundtrack that speeds up the longer your wheelie lasts.
  • Engine sound and vibration feedback.
  • Small download, plays offline, free with occasional ads between runs and never during a ride.
